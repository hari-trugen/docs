Below you will find answers to the most common questions about Clara SDR — from how Clara compares to a chatbot, to setup time, pricing, and security certifications. If you have a question that is not covered here, reach out at [clarasdr.ai](https://clarasdr.ai).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does SDR stand for in sales?">
    SDR stands for Sales Development Representative. An SDR qualifies inbound leads, conducts initial outreach, and books discovery calls for account executives. Clara automates this function with AI, handling every inbound visitor through a real face-to-face video conversation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is Clara different from a chatbot?">
    A chatbot answers predefined questions and routes visitors to forms. Clara conducts real sales conversations — qualifying leads, handling objections, running live product demos, and booking meetings directly into your team's calendar. Clara engages through face-to-face video, not a text bubble.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can Clara replace human SDRs?">
    Clara augments your human SDRs rather than replacing them. She handles high-volume repetitive inbound work — engagement, qualification, and booking — at a scale that humans cannot match. Your human SDRs can then focus on complex conversations, strategic outbound, and relationship-intensive selling. Clara delivers warmer, better-qualified leads to your team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    Most teams are live within hours. Choose an avatar, train Clara on your product knowledge base, and embed a single script tag on your website. Unlike enterprise platforms that require 30–60 day implementations, Clara is built for fast deployment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Clara secure enough for enterprise?">
    Yes. Clara is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, HIPAA compliant, and ISO 27001 certified. These certifications meet the security requirements for regulated industries including healthcare and financial services. Self-hosting and dedicated deployments are also available for Enterprise customers. See [Security](/security) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What CRM integrations does Clara support?">
    Clara natively integrates with HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, and Brevo. Enterprise customers can request custom CRM integrations to connect Clara to any system their team uses.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between inbound and outbound AI SDR?">
    An inbound AI SDR like Clara engages visitors who are already on your website, converting existing traffic into qualified leads and booked meetings. An outbound AI SDR automates cold prospecting — finding new contacts and sending cold outreach. Clara is inbound. The two categories solve different problems and are often deployed together.
  </Accordion>
