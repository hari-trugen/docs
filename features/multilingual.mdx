Clara operates continuously — no business hours, no weekends, no holidays, no time zones. Every visitor who lands on your website gets a real conversation regardless of when they arrive or where they're based. Human SDRs miss after-hours visitors and international prospects by default. Clara doesn't.

## Always-on availability

Clara never goes offline. She engages the first visitor at 2 AM the same way she engages the hundredth visitor at 2 PM on a Tuesday. There's no shift handoff, no out-of-office message, and no delay caused by time zone differences.

This matters most for:

- **After-hours traffic** — a significant share of B2B website visits happen outside the 9–5 window, especially from executives and senior buyers who research outside working hours
- **Global campaigns** — if you're running paid campaigns in APAC, EMEA, or LATAM, your SDR team is almost certainly asleep when those visitors arrive
- **Self-serve researchers** — buyers who prefer to evaluate on their own schedule, not yours

## Multilingual support

Clara automatically detects the language a visitor is using and responds in that language. You don't configure language rules, upload translations, or maintain separate playbooks per locale. Clara handles language detection and switching in real time.

<Note>
  Language detection is automatic — no additional setup is required. Clara detects the visitor's language from the first message and maintains that language for the entire conversation.
</Note>

<Info>
  Clara supports all major languages, including: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Italian, Dutch, Swedish, Korean, Mandarin Chinese, Arabic, and more. If your visitors speak it, Clara speaks it.
</Info>

## Concurrent session limits

Clara can handle multiple visitor conversations simultaneously. The number of concurrent sessions depends on your plan.

<CardGroup cols={3}>

    Suitable for low-traffic sites or teams testing Clara before scaling. One active visitor conversation at a time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pay as You Go" icon="gauge">

    No cap on simultaneous conversations. Designed for high-traffic sites, global campaigns, and teams with significant inbound volume.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## When to upgrade your concurrent session limit

If your concurrent session limit is too low for your actual traffic, Clara queues or declines new visitors when the limit is reached. You'll see this in your Dashboard as missed sessions.

Consider upgrading if:

- You're running paid acquisition campaigns that drive traffic spikes
- You have international audiences visiting across multiple time zones simultaneously
- Your site sees high weekend or off-hours traffic volumes
- You're launching a campaign or attending an event that will drive a surge

You can monitor concurrent session utilization in **Dashboard → Analytics → Capacity**. If you're regularly approaching your limit, upgrade before the next campaign rather than after.
