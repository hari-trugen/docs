Clara doesn't just qualify visitors and hand them off — she can show them what your product does, right in the conversation. When a visitor signals genuine interest, Clara can share her screen, walk through a pitch deck, navigate your live website, or display ROI models and case studies. Every demo adapts to who is watching.

## How screen-share demos work

Clara monitors the conversation for intent signals — questions about pricing, feature comparisons, "how does it work" phrasing, or explicit requests to see the product. When she detects sufficient interest, she initiates a screen share directly within the video conversation window.

The visitor doesn't need to install anything or navigate to a new tab. Clara presents materials inline, narrates what she's showing, and continues the conversation simultaneously.

<Note>
  Screen sharing combined with live browser navigation is unique to Clara in the AI SDR market. Most AI SDR tools are limited to text or pre-recorded video. Clara demonstrates your product live, on demand, for every visitor.
</Note>

## Context-aware personalization

No two visitors get the same demo. Clara adapts the demo flow based on:

- **Industry** — a fintech visitor sees relevant compliance and security content; a retail visitor sees conversion and customer engagement angles
- **Role** — a CTO gets the architecture walkthrough; a VP of Sales gets the pipeline impact and ROI story
- **Stated needs** — if a visitor mentions a specific pain point during qualification, Clara prioritizes the demo content that addresses it directly
- **Objections raised** — if the visitor mentions a competitor or a concern, Clara adjusts the demo to address that angle

## Objection handling during demos

Clara is trained on your sales playbook. When a visitor raises an objection mid-demo — "we already use [competitor X]," "this looks complex to set up," "our team won't adopt this" — Clara responds with the trained counter-messaging you've defined, then continues the demo.

You configure objection responses in **Dashboard → SDR Settings → Objection Handling**. Clara applies them in real time without breaking the flow of the conversation.

## Setting up demo content

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Demo Content in the Dashboard">
    Go to **Dashboard → SDR Settings → Demo Content**. This is where you manage all materials Clara can present.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your pitch deck">
    Upload your sales deck as a PDF or PowerPoint file. Add tags for industry or role so Clara knows when to use it. You can upload multiple versions for different audience segments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add supporting materials">
    Upload case studies, ROI models, architecture diagrams, and any other assets. Tag each file with the visitor profile it's most relevant to — industry, company size, role, or use case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure live URL navigation">
    Enter the URLs Clara is allowed to navigate to during demos — pricing page, feature pages, documentation sections. Clara will not navigate to pages outside this list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set demo trigger criteria">
    Define the intent signals that prompt Clara to initiate a demo — for example, specific qualifying questions answered, a minimum engagement score, or an explicit visitor request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a preview session">
    Use the **Preview** mode in the Dashboard to run a test conversation and verify that Clara selects and presents the right materials for different visitor profiles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Keep decks concise">
    Aim for 8–12 slides maximum. Long decks cause visitors to disengage. Clara can always go deeper with a follow-up conversation or meeting — the goal of the demo is to build enough interest to book the next step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Include a pricing slide">
    Visitors who reach the demo stage are actively evaluating. Include a pricing overview slide so Clara can address cost questions in context rather than deflecting to a follow-up call.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add case studies for your top verticals">
    Upload at least one case study per major industry you serve. Clara selects the closest match to the visitor's company type. A relevant customer story is often the most effective demo moment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Refresh content regularly">
    Update your pitch deck and case studies each quarter. Stale numbers or outdated positioning undermine trust when Clara presents them as current.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
