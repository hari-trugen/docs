When a visitor lands on your website, Clara begins a workflow that covers every stage of the inbound sales cycle: engagement, qualification, demonstration, objection handling, meeting booking, and CRM sync. Each stage happens within a single continuous conversation, so the visitor never has to repeat themselves or wait for a follow-up. This page explains what Clara does at each stage and how it compares to the traditional approach.

## The full inbound workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visitor engagement">
    Clara initiates a conversation as soon as a visitor arrives, but she does not deliver a generic greeting. She reads the context of the visit — which page the visitor is on, how they arrived (organic search, a paid ad, a specific campaign URL), and any signals available from prior sessions — and opens with a message that is relevant to that context.

    A visitor who lands on your pricing page after clicking a competitor comparison ad gets a different opening than someone browsing your features page from organic search. This context-aware engagement makes the first impression feel like a knowledgeable SDR, not an automated pop-up.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Lead qualification">
    Clara qualifies visitors through natural spoken conversation — no forms, no dropdowns, no checkbox surveys. She asks the questions your SDRs would ask: what brought them to the site today, what they are trying to solve, what their current setup looks like, and where they are in their buying process.

    During qualification, Clara captures:

    - **Role and seniority** — who she is speaking with and their decision-making authority
    - **Company size and industry** — firmographic context for your sales team
    - **Pain points and use case** — what the visitor is trying to accomplish
    - **Buying intent and timeline** — how actively they are evaluating solutions

    All of this goes into CRM enrichment automatically — your team sees a complete qualification record, not just a name and email.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Product demonstration">
    If a visitor wants to see the product, Clara does not send them to a pre-recorded walkthrough. She shares her screen mid-conversation and navigates your product, website, or presentation in real time.

    Clara can:

    - Present slides from your pitch deck
    - Navigate to specific pages on your marketing site or app
    - Walk through a feature relevant to the visitor's stated pain point
    - Answer questions about what she is showing without breaking the flow of the demo

    Because the demo happens inside the same conversation as qualification, Clara already knows what to show and why it matters to this specific visitor.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Objection handling">
    Clara is trained on your sales playbook, which means she handles objections with the responses your best SDRs use — not generic deflections. Common objections like pricing concerns, competitor comparisons, integration questions, and security requirements all receive specific, informed answers.

    You provide the objection-response pairs when you train Clara, and you can update them at any time from your dashboard. Clara uses these responses naturally in context rather than reciting them verbatim.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Meeting booking">
    When a visitor is ready to talk to your team, Clara books the meeting directly — without the visitor having to leave the conversation to find a calendar link. She checks your account executives' availability in real time and confirms a time that works.

    The visitor receives a calendar invite before they leave your website. Your AE receives the meeting with a full briefing: who the prospect is, what they care about, what objections they raised, and what Clara demonstrated. No handoff email, no lost context.
  </Step>

  <Step title="CRM sync">
    Every Clara conversation syncs automatically to your CRM — HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, or Brevo. The sync includes:

    - Full conversation summary
    - Lead qualification data (role, company, pain points, intent)
    - Company firmographics
    - Intent signals and topics discussed
    - Meeting booking confirmation and AE assignment

    Your team sees the full picture the moment the conversation ends. No manual data entry, no lost notes, no context gaps when the AE picks up the relationship.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Clara vs the traditional inbound approach

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="With Clara">
    A visitor lands on your pricing page at 11 PM on a Friday. Clara greets them with a relevant opening based on the page and their traffic source. Over the next 12 minutes, Clara qualifies them, shares her screen to run a live demo of the two features they asked about, answers a pricing objection with your standard response, and books a call with an AE for Monday morning. The visitor gets a calendar invite before they close the tab. The AE wakes up Monday to a fully briefed meeting — with a transcript, qualification summary, and CRM record already created.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Traditional approach">
    The same visitor lands on your pricing page at 11 PM on a Friday. A chat bubble appears with a bot that asks them to fill out a form. They ignore it and leave. Or they fill out the form, receive an automated email Monday morning, and by then they have already started a trial with a competitor who had a human SDR available. If they do respond to the email, the SDR has no context beyond their name and company — qualification starts over from scratch.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
