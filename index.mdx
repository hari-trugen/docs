## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your AI SDR">
    Sign up and design your Clara avatar — upload a face or generate one instantly with a prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Train Clara on your product">
    Upload pitch decks, sales scripts, product docs, and FAQs so Clara learns your messaging and speaks your brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch on your website">
    Embed a single script tag on your website or landing page and go live within 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch pipeline grow">
    Clara qualifies visitors, runs demos, and books meetings automatically — while your team focuses on closing.
  </Step>
</Steps>
