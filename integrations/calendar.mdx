Once you connect a calendar, Clara handles the entire booking flow on your behalf. When a prospect qualifies, Clara presents available times, the prospect picks a slot, and they receive a calendar invite — all before leaving your website. Your AE gets the meeting on their calendar along with full lead context already synced to your CRM.

## How booking works

1. Clara qualifies the prospect based on your configured criteria.
2. Clara surfaces a scheduling interface directly in the conversation — the prospect never navigates away.
3. The prospect selects a date and time from your team's live availability.
4. Both the prospect and the assigned AE receive a calendar invite instantly.
5. Meeting details sync to your CRM so the AE has full context when they join.

## Connect your calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the integrations page">
    In your Clara dashboard, navigate to **Integrations → Calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your calendar provider">
    Click **Calendly**, **Google Calendar**, or **Outlook Calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the connection">
    You are redirected to your calendar provider to grant Clara read access to availability and write access to create events. Sign in and confirm the permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure routing rules">
    After connecting, set up routing rules to control which AE receives each meeting. Route by territory, vertical, company size, or any combination of Clara's qualification fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test a booking">
    Use the **Test booking** button in the dashboard to simulate a prospect scheduling a meeting and confirm the invite appears on the correct calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use Calendly if your team has complex availability rules, round-robin scheduling, or territory-based routing across many AEs. Calendly's routing logic integrates cleanly with Clara's qualification output and gives you the most flexibility.
</Tip>

## Routing rules

Routing rules let you send qualified meetings to the right AE automatically. You can create rules based on:

- **Territory** — route by country, region, or zip code
- **Company size** — route enterprise vs. SMB leads to different AEs
- **Product line** — route based on the product the prospect expressed interest in
- **Vertical** — route by industry or segment

You can connect multiple AE calendars and assign each one a priority order within a routing rule. If the top-priority AE has no availability, Clara moves to the next AE in the list.

<Info>
  Changes to routing rules take effect immediately. Meetings already booked are not affected by rule updates.
</Info>
