When Clara qualifies a prospect or books a meeting, your team can know about it the moment it happens. Connect Slack or Microsoft Teams to receive real-time notifications that include everything your AEs need to act fast — conversation summary, lead data, company info, and meeting time.

## What you receive

Every notification Clara sends includes:

- **Conversation summary** — what the prospect said, their pain points, and their intent
- **Lead data** — name, title, company, and email
- **Company info** — industry, employee count, and any firmographic data Clara collected
- **Meeting time** — date, time, assigned AE, and meeting link (for booked-meeting notifications)

Your team can react to hot leads the moment they emerge — even outside business hours.

## Connect your communication tool

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Slack">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the integrations page">
        In your Clara dashboard, navigate to **Integrations → Communication** and click **Slack**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authorize with OAuth">
        You are redirected to Slack. Sign in to your workspace and grant Clara permission to post messages to channels.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select a notification channel">
        Choose the Slack channel where Clara should send lead and booking notifications. You can select a different channel for qualified-lead alerts and booked-meeting alerts, or send both to the same channel.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Test the connection">
        Click **Send test notification** to confirm a sample alert appears in your selected channel.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Microsoft Teams">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the integrations page">
        In your Clara dashboard, navigate to **Integrations → Communication** and click **Microsoft Teams**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authorize with OAuth">
        You are redirected to Microsoft. Sign in with your Microsoft 365 account and grant Clara permission to post messages to Teams channels.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select a notification channel">
        Choose the Teams channel where Clara should post alerts. You can configure separate channels for qualified-lead notifications and booked-meeting notifications.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Test the connection">
        Click **Send test notification** to confirm a sample message appears in your selected channel.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

On Enterprise plans, Clara can communicate with prospects directly via Slack or Microsoft Teams — not just send internal notifications to your team. This allows Clara to engage, qualify, and book meetings with prospects who prefer asynchronous messaging over a website video conversation.
