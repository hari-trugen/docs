Clara SDR is an AI Sales Development Representative that works directly on your website — not as a text chat bubble, but as a face-to-face video agent that greets visitors, holds real conversations, demonstrates your product live, handles objections, and books meetings into your sales team's calendar around the clock. The problem Clara solves is straightforward: 97% of website visitors leave without ever converting. Clara intercepts that drop-off by providing the kind of personalized, immediate engagement that previously required a human SDR to be available at all hours.

## Clara is not a chatbot

Traditional chatbots engage visitors through text, rely on decision trees or keyword matching, and funnel people into static forms. Clara takes a fundamentally different approach: she appears as a video presence on your website, initiates a natural spoken conversation based on the page the visitor is viewing, and qualifies them the same way a skilled SDR would — by listening, asking the right questions, and responding to what she hears.

## Inbound AI SDR vs outbound AI SDR

You may have encountered AI tools that automate outbound prospecting — writing cold emails, sequencing LinkedIn messages, or generating call scripts. Clara is different: she is an **inbound** AI SDR. Her job is to engage visitors who have already arrived on your website and shown interest in your product. These are your highest-intent leads, and Clara ensures none of them leave without a conversation.

<Info>
  Clara does not send cold outreach. She works exclusively with visitors who arrive on your website — the leads most likely to convert.
</Info>

## Who Clara is built for

Clara delivers the most value for B2B companies that meet a few conditions:

- **Meaningful website traffic** — at least 1,000 monthly visitors, so there is a steady stream of inbound interest for Clara to engage
- **A product that benefits from a demo** — SaaS products, platforms, and tools where a live walkthrough meaningfully accelerates purchase decisions
- **A need for 24/7 coverage** — global audiences, asynchronous buying cycles, or simply more conversations than your SDR team can handle alone

<Note>
  If your team is already running live demos and qualifying inbound leads manually, Clara extends that capacity without adding headcount.
</Note>

## What Clara does

Clara handles the full inbound sales cycle from first contact to booked meeting:

- **Qualifies leads** through natural conversation — capturing role, company size, pain points, and buying intent without a form
- **Runs live product demos** using screen share and real-time browser navigation, mid-conversation
- **Handles objections** using responses trained on your specific sales playbook
- **Books meetings** directly into your account executives' calendars, with the prospect receiving a calendar invite before they leave your site
- **Syncs to your CRM** — full conversation summaries, qualification data, and intent signals to HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, or Brevo
