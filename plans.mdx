Clara offers three plans designed to scale with your website traffic and sales goals. There are no hidden fees and no long-term contracts on the Starter and Pay as You Go plans — you can start free and upgrade when you're ready.

<Tip>
  The free Starter plan requires no credit card. You can be live on your website within hours.
</Tip>

## Plans

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Starter" icon="seedling">
    **$0 / month**

    Best for websites with fewer than 10,000 monthly visitors.

    - 10 buyer conversations per month
    - Limited buyer simulations
    - Present slides, images, and videos
    - 1 concurrent session
    - No credit card required
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pay as You Go" icon="bolt">
    **$299 / month**

    Best for websites with 10,000–100,000 monthly visitors.

    - 300 conversations per month included
    - $1 per additional buyer conversation
    - Book meetings automatically
    - Run live product demos
    - Custom CRM integrations
    - Customer support via Slack
    - 20 concurrent sessions
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enterprise" icon="building">
    **Custom pricing**

    Best for websites with 100,000+ monthly visitors.

    - Unlimited conversations
    - Unlimited concurrent sessions
    - Custom avatar and voices
    - White-labeled experience
    - Custom CRM and data integrations
    - AI SDR joins Zoom, Teams, and other meetings
    - AI SDR communicates via Slack and Teams
    - Feature prioritization and custom development
    - Self-hosting and dedicated deployments
    - Enterprise-grade security and compliance
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  On the Pay as You Go plan, each buyer conversation beyond the included 300 is billed at $1. You are only charged for what you use.
</Note>
