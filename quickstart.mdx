Getting Clara live on your website takes three steps: create an avatar, train Clara on your product, and paste a single embed script onto your site. There is no infrastructure to manage, no integration work required before launch, and no credit card required to start. The steps below walk you through the full setup process.

<Note>
  Clara's free plan includes 10 buyer conversations per month with no credit card required. Sign up at [clarasdr.ai](https://clarasdr.ai) to get started.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your AI SDR">
    Sign up at [clarasdr.ai](https://clarasdr.ai) and design your Clara avatar. You have two options:

    - **Upload a face** — provide a photo of a real person (yourself, a team member, or a brand-approved image) and Clara will animate it as your video SDR
    - **Generate with a prompt** — describe the look and style you want, and Clara generates an avatar for you instantly

    Once your avatar is created, give your SDR a name and configure her greeting — the opening line she uses when a visitor lands on your site.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Train Clara on your product">
    Clara learns your product, messaging, and sales process from the materials you upload. Add as much or as little as you have:

    - Pitch decks and slide presentations
    - Sales scripts and call talk tracks
    - Product documentation and feature guides
    - FAQs and common objections with your preferred responses
    - Pricing pages and competitor comparison sheets

    Clara reads, understands, and synthesizes these materials so she can answer questions, run demos, and handle objections in your brand voice. You can update training materials at any time from your dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch Clara on your website">
    Copy the embed script from your Clara dashboard and paste it before the closing `</body>` tag on any page where you want Clara to appear.

    ```html
    <script
      src="https://cdn.clarasdr.ai/embed.js"
      data-sdr-id="YOUR_SDR_ID"
      async
    ></script>
    ```

    Replace `YOUR_SDR_ID` with the ID shown in your Clara dashboard. Clara will appear automatically when a visitor lands on the page — no additional configuration required.

    <Tip>
      Most teams complete setup and go live within a few hours. You do not need to finish training before launching — Clara improves as you add more materials over time.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After launch

Once Clara is live, she handles visitor engagement automatically. From your dashboard you can:

- **Review conversation recordings** — watch or read full transcripts of every Clara session
- **Monitor qualification data** — see the role, company size, pain points, and intent signals Clara captures
- **Check your CRM** — qualification data and conversation summaries sync automatically to HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, or Brevo
- **Refine training** — add new materials or adjust objection responses based on what you see in conversations
