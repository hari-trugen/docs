Security is central to how Clara is built. Enterprise customers in healthcare, financial services, and other regulated industries rely on Clara to handle buyer conversations and lead data safely. Clara meets the security and compliance requirements of the most demanding procurement teams, and offers self-hosting options for organizations that need complete control over their infrastructure.

## Certifications

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SOC 2 Type II" icon="shield-check">
    Clara has completed an independent SOC 2 Type II audit, confirming that security controls for availability, confidentiality, and data protection are in place and operating effectively. This certification is required by many enterprise procurement and vendor security reviews.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GDPR" icon="lock">
    Clara adheres to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the EU's advanced privacy framework for the protection of personal data. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available for customers who require one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HIPAA" icon="heart-pulse">
    Clara is certified for secure handling of protected health information (PHI) under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Healthcare organizations can deploy Clara to engage patients and prospects without compromising compliance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ISO 27001" icon="medal">
    Clara is certified to ISO 27001, the internationally recognized standard for information security management systems. This certification demonstrates a systematic, risk-based approach to securing customer and company data.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Data handling

All conversations and lead data are encrypted in transit and at rest. Clara does not sell or share your data with third parties.

## Enterprise deployment options

Enterprise customers can choose between cloud-hosted and self-hosted deployments:

- **Cloud-hosted**: Clara manages infrastructure, updates, and uptime in a dedicated environment.
- **Self-hosting**: Deploy Clara entirely within your own infrastructure for maximum control and data residency compliance.

Contact sales to discuss the right deployment model for your organization.

<Note>
  A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available for GDPR compliance. Contact [clarasdr.ai](https://clarasdr.ai) to request one.
</Note>
