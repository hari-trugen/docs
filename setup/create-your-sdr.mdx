---

Before Clara can start engaging your website visitors, you need to create her. This takes about five minutes: sign up, design an avatar, pick a voice, and you're ready to move on to training. You can update any of these settings later, so don't worry about getting everything perfect on the first pass.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up at clarasdr.ai">
    Go to [clarasdr.ai](https://clarasdr.ai) and create your account. The free plan requires no credit card and gives you 10 buyer conversations per month to get started.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Create New SDR">
    From your dashboard, click **Create New SDR**. This opens the SDR setup wizard, which walks you through avatar design, naming, and voice selection.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your avatar">
    Clara offers two ways to set your SDR's face:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Upload a photo" icon="image">
        Upload a clear, professional headshot of a real person. Use a photo with good lighting, a neutral background, and a forward-facing expression.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Generate with a prompt" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
        Describe the look you want and Clara generates a realistic avatar instantly. No photo needed.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    <Tip>
      If your team doesn't have a specific face in mind, the generated avatar option is the fastest path forward. Try a prompt like "professional woman in her 30s, friendly and confident" to get a polished, brand-appropriate result in seconds.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name your SDR and select a voice">
    Give your SDR a name — this is the name she uses when introducing herself to visitors. Then choose a voice style from the available options. Voices vary in tone, pace, and accent, so preview a few before committing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview your avatar">
    Use the built-in preview to see and hear your SDR before saving. Check that the voice style matches your brand tone and that the avatar looks professional. Adjust the name, voice, or avatar if anything feels off.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your SDR">
    Click **Save**. Your SDR is created and appears in your dashboard. From here, you can move on to training Clara on your product and sales playbook.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can change your avatar photo, generated face, name, or voice at any time from **Dashboard → Your SDR → Settings**. Changes take effect immediately.
</Note>
