Once Clara is trained, deploying her to your website takes a single script tag and a copy-paste. No RevOps involvement, no complex integrations, and no waiting on a developer queue. Most teams go from trained to live in under an hour. Clara appears as a video widget that greets visitors automatically and starts qualifying them in real time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your embed script">
    Go to **Dashboard → Your SDR → Embed**. You'll see your unique embed script with your SDR ID pre-filled. Click **Copy** to copy it to your clipboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste the script into your website">
    Add the script to every page where you want Clara to appear, just before the closing `</body>` tag. The exact method depends on your platform:

    <CodeGroup>

    ```html HTML
    <!-- Paste before the closing </body> tag -->
    <script
      src="https://cdn.clarasdr.ai/embed.js"
      data-sdr-id="YOUR_SDR_ID"
      async
    ></script>
    </body>
    ```

    ```php WordPress
    // Option 1: Use a header/footer plugin (e.g., Insert Headers and Footers)
    // Paste the script tag into the "Footer" field.

    // Option 2: Add to your theme's functions.php
    function clara_sdr_embed() {
        echo '<script src="https://cdn.clarasdr.ai/embed.js" data-sdr-id="YOUR_SDR_ID" async></script>';
    }
    add_action( 'wp_footer', 'clara_sdr_embed' );
    ```

    ```html Webflow
    <!-- In Webflow: go to Site Settings → Custom Code → Footer Code -->
    <!-- Paste the script tag into the footer code field and publish your site -->
    <script
      src="https://cdn.clarasdr.ai/embed.js"
      data-sdr-id="YOUR_SDR_ID"
      async
    ></script>
    ```

    </CodeGroup>

    <Warning>
      The script tag must load on the page before Clara can engage visitors. If you place it in the `<head>` or block it with a content security policy, Clara will not appear. Always add the script before the closing `</body>` tag and ensure your CSP allows scripts from `cdn.clarasdr.ai`.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Target specific pages (optional)">
    By default, Clara appears on every page where the script is installed. If you want Clara on specific pages only — such as your homepage or a high-intent landing page — you have two options:

    - **Selective install**: Add the script only to the pages you want Clara on, rather than site-wide.
    - **Page targeting rules**: Use the **Targeting** settings in **Dashboard → Your SDR → Embed** to define URL patterns. Clara will only activate on pages that match your rules.

    Targeted deployments are common for teams running paid campaigns who want Clara on specific landing pages without affecting the rest of their site.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Clara is live">
    After publishing your changes, open the page in a browser and wait a few seconds. Clara's video widget should appear and greet you automatically.

    To run a test conversation without affecting your analytics, use **Preview Mode** in **Dashboard → Your SDR → Embed**. Preview Mode loads a sandboxed version of Clara you can interact with before your changes go live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Enterprise customers can request self-hosted or dedicated deployment options, including private CDN hosting and isolated infrastructure. Contact your account team to discuss deployment requirements.
</Note>
