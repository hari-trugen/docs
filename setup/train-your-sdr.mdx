---
title: "Train Clara SDR on Your Product and Sales Playbook"
sidebarTitle: "Train Your SDR"
description: "Upload pitch decks, sales scripts, FAQs, and product docs so Clara learns your messaging, value props, and how to handle objections."
---

Clara learns everything she needs from the materials you already have. Upload your existing pitch decks, sales scripts, and product documentation and Clara will absorb your brand voice, product value propositions, pricing structure, and objection-handling playbook — no manual configuration required. Training takes minutes, and Clara is ready to engage visitors as soon as it completes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Training section">
    From your dashboard, go to **Your SDR → Training**. This is where you manage all the materials Clara learns from.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload your training materials">
    Click **Upload Files** and select the documents you want Clara to learn from. Clara accepts the following file types:

    | Format | Best for |
    |--------|----------|
    | PDF | Pitch decks, one-pagers, case studies, battlecards |
    | PPTX | Slide decks and presentation materials |
    | DOCX | Sales scripts, FAQs, product documentation |
    | TXT | Plain-text playbooks, talking points, objection guides |

    You can upload multiple files at once. There is no strict limit on the number of documents.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Include the right content">
    The quality of Clara's conversations depends directly on the quality and specificity of your training materials. Aim to upload at least:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Core product content" icon="box">
        Pitch decks, product one-pagers, and feature documentation that explain what you sell and why it matters.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Sales playbook" icon="book">
        Sales scripts, FAQ documents, and objection-handling guides that reflect how your best reps handle real conversations.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Social proof" icon="star">
        Case studies and customer stories that Clara can reference when prospects ask for proof of results.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Competitive intelligence" icon="chart-bar">
        Competitor battlecards so Clara can address comparisons and position your product accurately.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    <Tip>
      The more specific your sales playbook, the better Clara handles edge-case objections. If your reps have documented responses to unusual or difficult questions, include those documents. Generic FAQs alone won't cover the full range of real buyer conversations.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for training to complete">
    Once you upload your files, Clara processes and indexes them automatically. Training typically completes within a few minutes. You'll see a confirmation in the Training section when Clara is ready. No action is required on your part during this time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Clara's knowledge (optional)">
    Use the **Test Conversation** tool in the Training section to ask Clara questions about your product. This lets you spot gaps — if Clara can't answer a question your buyers commonly ask, upload a document that addresses it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On paid plans, Clara continuously improves from real conversations she has with your website visitors. She learns from buyer questions and responses over time, becoming more accurate and effective the more conversations she has.
</Note>

<Info>
  You can update your training materials at any time. Upload new documents or remove outdated ones from **Dashboard → Your SDR → Training** and Clara re-learns automatically. Use this to keep Clara current when you launch new products, adjust pricing, or update your messaging.
</Info>
